Privacy notice · Notis privasi

How we handle your data.

Last updated 24 September 2026 · Applies to eiaawsolutions.com

English · Bahasa Malaysia

This notice explains what personal data EIAAW Solutions collects through this website, why, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It is issued under Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (as amended in 2024) and written to also meet the privacy laws of the other Asia-Pacific countries our visitors come from, including Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong and India. A Bahasa Malaysia version follows; both versions have the same meaning.

Each EIAAW product (Sales Agent, Ai Ads Agency, Workforce, Social Media Team) has its own terms and privacy notice on its own site. This notice covers this website only.

1. Who we are

EIAAW SOLUTIONS, registered in Malaysia (SSM 202603133419, CT0164540-H), Kuala Lumpur, is responsible for your personal data on this website. Contact our Data Protection Officer at eiaawsolutions@gmail.com (subject “DPO”) about anything in this notice.

2. What we collect, why, and on what basis

  • Enquiry form (“Talk to us”). Name, email and message are required so we can reply; phone and company are optional. Purpose: to answer your enquiry and follow up on it. Basis: your consent, given by ticking the box on the form.
  • Chat assistant. Before the assistant answers, we ask for your name, email and phone (company is optional) so our team can follow up. Your chat messages are sent to an AI model provider to generate replies. Basis: your consent, given by ticking the box before you chat.
  • Voice agent (“Talk to the agent”). Starting a call opens a session with our voice AI provider, and what you say is processed to hold the conversation. The agent always identifies itself as AI. Basis: your consent, given by choosing to start the call.
  • Analytics and ad measurement. Only if you allow them: Google Analytics tells us which pages and channels bring visitors and enquiries, and the Meta Pixel measures our Facebook and Instagram ads. They receive technical data such as pages viewed, device, browser and approximate location, never the contents of your forms or chats. Basis: your consent through the cookie choices.
  • Security logs. Our hosting and network providers log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the site secure and working. Basis: our legitimate interest in security, and legal obligations.

If you don’t give us the required details, we can’t reply to your enquiry or start a chat — everything else on the site still works. Please don’t share sensitive personal data (such as health, financial or identity-document details) in forms or chats.

3. AI and automated processing

Chat replies are written by AI; follow-ups on your enquiry are handled by people on our team. We don’t make any decision with legal or similarly significant effects about you by automated means alone.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Nothing that tracks you loads until you choose. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page).

5. Who we share it with

We don’t sell your personal data or share it with anyone for their own marketing. We share it only with:

  • our own CRM, which runs on the EIAAW Sales Agent platform, and our team inbox;
  • service providers that work for us: hosting and networking (Railway, Cloudflare), email delivery, the AI model provider behind the chat assistant, the voice AI provider behind the voice agent, and — only with your consent — Google (analytics) and Meta (advertising);
  • authorities, courts or regulators when the law requires it.

6. Transfers outside your country

Our providers process data outside Malaysia and outside your own country, including in Singapore and the United States. We use providers bound by data-protection terms that protect your data to a standard comparable to Malaysian law, and where your local law requires consent for such transfers, we ask for it on the form.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and chat details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them — unless you become a customer (then that product’s terms apply) or the law requires us to keep them longer. Analytics data is kept according to the cookie durations above.

8. Security

This site is served over HTTPS only, and access to enquiry data is limited to the EIAAW team members who need it to respond. If a data breach is likely to harm you, we will notify the relevant regulator and you as the law requires — in Malaysia, the Personal Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours.

9. Your rights

  • See the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy (including in a portable, machine-readable format).
  • Correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, object to or ask us to limit processing, and stop us contacting you. Withdrawing doesn’t affect what we did before.
  • Complain to your data protection regulator (see below).

Email eiaawsolutions@gmail.com with “Personal data request” in the subject. We’ll verify it’s you, then respond within 21 days, or sooner where your local law requires. There is no charge unless the law allows one for repeated requests, and we’ll tell you first. We don’t send marketing unless you ask for it, and every marketing message lets you opt out.

10. Regulators

You can contact us first, and you can also complain to your regulator: Malaysia — Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP); Singapore — Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC); Indonesia — Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi); Thailand — Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC); Philippines — National Privacy Commission (NPC); Vietnam — Ministry of Public Security (A05); Australia — Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC); New Zealand — Office of the Privacy Commissioner; Japan — Personal Information Protection Commission (PPC); South Korea — Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC); Hong Kong — Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD); India — Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children

This website is for businesses and isn’t directed at anyone under 18. We don’t knowingly collect children’s personal data; if you think we have, contact us and we’ll delete it.

12. Changes

If we change how we handle personal data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what you consented to, we’ll ask again.
