Meet CoS

The calm operator behind every decision. A friendly face with a shield at heart, two helping hands, and three little satellites that do the legwork.

Design rationale

  • Soft shield head — the EIAAW shield, rounded and glossy: trustworthy without looking cold.
  • Cream face, big eyes — warm, expressive, always smiling. Blush cheeks use the brand gold.
  • Helping hands — floating mitts that wave hello. CoS is approachable, not a screen.
  • Three satellites — Brief, Schedule, Decide: the logo's neural nodes as little helpers.
  • Gold antenna — a bouncing gold orb is the CoS signature; it glows when work is under way.

Palette (from brand tokens)

Deep Dark Teal Glow Gold

Behaviour states

  • Standing by — waves hello, bounces, blinks, gaze follows the cursor.
  • Listening — leans in, eyes widen, satellites draw close.
  • Thinking — eyes look up, satellites turn gold and spin.
  • Speaking — mouth opens with the voice, ring pulses.

Build notes

Procedural Three.js (no external models, ~0 KB assets), smooth-shaded glossy materials. Same rig drives a Lottie/Rive 2D version or a GLB export for Unity, Unreal or WebXR.
