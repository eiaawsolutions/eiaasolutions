Meet CoS

The calm operator behind every decision. A faceted crystal-shield head, a waveform voice, and three satellites that do the legwork.

Design rationale

  • Shield head — lifted straight from the EIAAW mark: trust, governance, protection.
  • Waveform mouth — CoS is a voice first. The mouth is a live audio meter, not a cartoon smile.
  • Three satellites — Brief, Schedule, Decide. The neural nodes from the logo, now working agents that orbit and report back.
  • Tailored bust — ink blazer, cream lapels, a teal pin. Executive, not toy-like; no face to uncanny-valley.
  • Gold = thinking — the only warm accent. When it appears, CoS is working on your behalf.

Palette (from brand tokens)

Deep Dark Teal Glow Gold

Behaviour states

  • Standing by — breathing, blinks, gaze follows the cursor.
  • Listening — leans in, eyes widen, satellites draw close.
  • Thinking — eyes narrow and look up, satellites spin gold.
  • Speaking — waveform mouth, ring pulses with voice.

Build notes

Procedural Three.js (no external models, ~0 KB assets), flat-shaded for the crystal look. Same rig drives a Lottie/Rive 2D version or a GLB export for Unity, Unreal or WebXR.
