The Automated Employee Journey

Onboard, manage, offboard — one continuous flow across HR, IT, and Accounting.

A new hire fills the invite form once. From that moment, every downstream system — HR records, IT assets, payroll, EA forms, statutory submissions, the general ledger — moves in step. No exports. No reconciliations. No SaaS sprawl.

Pillar 01 · HR & People Operations
01.

A full HRM suite — onboard, manage, offboard, and pay them right.

The employee journey runs end-to-end on one record. Section A–I of the invite link captures personal, work, education, dependent, and statutory data. AI auto-activates the account on start date, cascades into every relationship table, and runs the same chain in reverse on offboarding. Leave, attendance, payslips, EA forms, and government statutory submissions — EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB — all live on the same timeline.

  • Tokenised invite link with self-service NRIC + photo capture
  • Auto-activation on start date · auto-offboarding handoff to IT & Finance
  • Leave management with accruals, manager reminders, weekly pending-sweep
  • Attendance · payslips · EA forms · eClaim under one record
  • EPF / SOCSO / EIS / PCB calculated every payroll · statutory file exports for LHDN, KWSP, PERKESO, HRDC
  • Edit history with cryptographic re-acknowledgement on sensitive fields
Pillar 02 · IT Asset Management
02.

Every laptop, phone, and licence — tracked, signed, returned, automatically.

The moment HR confirms a start date, IT sees the request. Assets are picked from live inventory, assigned to the employee, and acknowledged through a signed Asset Acceptance & Return Form (AARF) sent by email — no spreadsheets, no Slack threads. On exit, the same chain runs in reverse: a return AARF is dispatched, the employee signs off, and the asset is unassigned and either restocked or booked into the disposed-asset register that feeds Finance depreciation.

  • Live asset inventory with full lifecycle history per unit
  • Auto acceptance AARF on assignment · auto return AARF on offboarding
  • Tokenised email acknowledgement with full audit trail
  • Provisioning workflows that wait for IT sign-off before payroll finalises
  • Disposed-asset register flows straight to Finance depreciation

Frequently asked

The five questions every operator asks first.

Direct answers, no marketing fluff. Full FAQ on the FAQ page.

What is EIAAW Workforce?

An AI-native platform that runs three departments — HR, IT, and Accounting — on a single multi-tenant backbone. It carries the full employee journey from onboarding through active management to offboarding, automates IT asset acceptance and return via signed AARF links, and posts approved claims and payroll directly to a full accounting ledger.

Which Malaysian statutory submissions are covered?

EPF, SOCSO, EIS, and PCB are calculated on every payroll run. EA forms are auto-generated for each employee at year-end. Statutory file exports are formatted for direct submission to LHDN, KWSP, PERKESO, and HRDC.

Is the AI assistant safe to use on real employee data?

Every AI answer is retrieval-grounded on your tenant only, cites the records it read, respects role-based access, and runs under a per-tenant cost circuit breaker so a runaway prompt never shocks your bill.

How is tenant data isolated?

EIAAW Workforce runs on Postgres with Row-Level Security enforcing tenant_id at the driver level. Cross-tenant data leaks are impossible even through a bugged controller.
