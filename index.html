FAQ

Straight answers, before you ask.

Questions · 01–10
01

What does EIAAW Solutions do?

EIAAW Solutions is an AI engineering and integration firm, based in Malaysia, focused on building production AI systems/agents and SaaS platforms for businesses.

Its current core product lineup:

  • EIAAW AI Sales Agent — sa.eiaawsolutions.com
  • AI Social Media Team (SMT) — smt.eiaawsolutions.com
  • AI Ads Agency — ads.eiaawsolutions.com
  • EIAAW Workforce/EP — ep.eiaawsolutions.com
02

Does EIAAW build custom AI systems and agents?

Yes. Beyond our own products, we design, build and integrate production AI systems and agents for businesses, connected to the tools and data you already use. Every build is held to the same seven ethical principles as our products, including human oversight. Tell us what you’re working on and we’ll scope it with you.

03

Which EIAAW product is right for my business?

Pick by the job you need done. Need more qualified sales pipeline? Sales Agent. Running paid ads on Google, Meta, TikTok or LinkedIn? Ai Ads Agency. Running HR, payroll, IT assets and accounting in Malaysia? Workforce. Need consistent, on-brand social posts without hiring a full team? Social Media Team. Need something none of them covers, or still not sure? Talk to us and we’ll give you an honest recommendation, including whether a custom build makes sense.

04

How much do EIAAW products cost?

Sales Agent: RM 99, RM 199 or RM 399 per month (Starter, Pro, Business). Ai Ads Agency: USD 499 per month (Starter) or USD 1,499 per month (Growth); Enterprise is custom. Workforce: USD 6, USD 14 or USD 29 per active employee per month (Starter, Growth, Scale); Enterprise is custom. Social Media Team: RM 688, RM 1,688 or RM 6,888 per month (Solo, Studio, Agency); Enterprise is bespoke. Custom AI systems and integrations are quoted per project.

05

Is there a free trial?

Workforce has a 14-day trial with no credit card. Sales Agent and Social Media Team are billed monthly at checkout with no free trial, and Sales Agent can be cancelled anytime. For Ai Ads Agency, start onboarding at ads.eiaawsolutions.com or talk to us.

06

Does the AI make the final decisions?

No. Every EIAAW product puts a human approval gate before anything externally visible happens. Sales Agent drafts outreach for your review before it’s sent. Ai Ads Agency needs sign-off on creatives and audit fixes. Workforce routes payroll, claims and ledger postings through role-based approvals. Social Media Team holds any post that fails one of its eight compliance checks, with the reason shown.

07

How does EIAAW handle my data?

Data is encrypted with TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest, and every customer is isolated with row-level tenant separation. Our practices are aligned with Malaysia’s PDPA, GDPR and CCPA, with clear data-residency choices. See our privacy notice for how this website handles enquiries.

08

Where is EIAAW based?

EIAAW Solutions is an AI engineering and integration firm registered in Malaysia (SSM 202603133419) and based in Kuala Lumpur. We work with businesses across Malaysia, Singapore and the wider Asia-Pacific.

09

What are EIAAW’s seven ethical principles?

Human Dignity First, Transparency, Fairness, Human Oversight, Privacy & Data, Continuous Learning, and True Partnership. Every implementation is measured against all seven.
